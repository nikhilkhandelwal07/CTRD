Palamalai Milk Slip

A voice slip for every pour of milk: the farmer hears litres, quality and money due on any phone, in Tamil, Malayalam, Hindi or English. CTRD sees everything on one dashboard.

Prototype · sample data

How it works for the farmer

  1. After milk is poured, the farmer's phone rings. They press the green key and hear the slip in their language.
  2. Any time, they can give a free missed call. The dairy calls back, asks for a language (1 Tamil, 2 Malayalam, 3 Hindi, 4 English) and reads their total.
  3. No reading is needed. Press 1 to hear again, 2 to hear the total.

What the farmer hears

Voice uses this device's built-in speech. If a language has no voice installed, the words still show on the phone screen.
